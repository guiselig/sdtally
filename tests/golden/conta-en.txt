Why space may not come back (measured only)
  Cloud-only files (2,802 files)                             55.62   scan
    Cloud-only files (iCloud Drive and other providers) do not use disk space:
    deleting them here gives nothing back. The number is the apparent size, not
    the space used.
  Purgeable                                                  36.71   CacheDelete
    Available in Settings is free plus purgeable. Purgeable only becomes free
    when macOS releases it; tools that show free space (df, Terminal) do not
    count it as free.
  Local snapshots (8)                                        32.18   fsctl
    A local snapshot still holds the blocks of files you deleted: the space only
    comes back when the snapshot is gone. macOS removes Time Machine ones on its
    own when space runs low.
  APFS clones (81,998 files)                                 26.21   scan
    An APFS clone shares blocks with the original file: deleting only one of
    them gives back little or nothing. The space comes back when the last one is
    gone.
